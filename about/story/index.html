BUILT FOR BETTER
HEALTHCARE TECH HIRING

Shoreline Recruitment Group was built to help healthcare technology companies solve critical hiring challenges through a more focused, strategic recruiting approach. We believe the best hiring outcomes come from stronger alignment, faster execution, and a search process built around long-term success—not just filling open roles.

Our experience across healthcare technology, revenue-generating roles, and complex business-critical hiring has shaped the way we work. We understand the pressure companies face when growth is outpacing hiring, revenue targets are at risk, or critical leadership roles remain open for too long.

That is why we built Shoreline Recruitment Group around an engaged contingent model—combining the flexibility of contingent recruiting with the focus, accountability, and partnership of a retained search firm.

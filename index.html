HOW WE WORK

ENGAGED CONTINGENT SEARCH
WITHOUT THE FULL RETAINER

We combine the flexibility of contingent recruiting with the focus, urgency, and accountability of a retained search partner—giving you faster access to stronger candidates without requiring a full retainer. Our engaged model ensures your search receives dedicated attention, priority support, and a more strategic hiring process from start to finish.

01

No Full Retainer Required

Keep the flexibility of a contingent model without committing to a large upfront fee.

02

Priority Access

An engagement fee secures dedicated focus, faster delivery, and priority search support.

03

Fee Credited Back

Higher-Quality Candidates

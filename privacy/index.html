Privacy Policy

Shoreline Recruitment Group respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information.

Information We Collect

We may collect personal information including your name, phone number, email address, company name, and message content when you submit a form on our website. We may also collect technical information such as IP address, device type, browser information, and website interaction data.

How Information Is Used

Information collected through this website is used solely to respond to inquiries, discuss recruitment and executive search engagements, communicate regarding your hiring needs, and deliver requested services. We may also use information to improve website performance and user experience.

SMS & Mobile Communications

By submitting your phone number through this website, you consent to receive phone calls and SMS text messages from Shoreline Recruitment Group regarding your inquiry, consultation scheduling, and related recruitment services.

Message frequency may vary. Message and data rates may apply.

You may opt out of SMS communications at any time by replying STOP.

Mobile Information Protection

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Information sharing with support service providers in direct support of our recruitment services is permitted. All other use case categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

Cookies & Analytics

This website may use cookies and analytics tools to understand website usage, improve user experience, and monitor website performance. You may disable cookies through your browser settings if desired.

Data Protection

We implement reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect your personal information. However, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information at any time by contacting us.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically. Continued use of this website constitutes acceptance of any updates.
